Software Supply Chain Assessment

Self-Assessment

A short structured questionnaire about how software artifacts are built, stored, secured and released in your organisation. Your answers produce a maturity scorecard and a prioritised 12-month roadmap.

How this works

There are nine domains. Each starts with a short capability check — statements you answer Yes, Partly, No, or Not sure with one tap. Those answers point you at the overall maturity level for the domain, which is the one choice that produces your score.

Answer for how things actually work now, not how they are meant to work. Partly is the honest answer more often than not — a control that covers containers but not npm, or a policy that exists but isn't enforced, is a Partly.

If you don't have visibility into something, choose Not sure. It is a legitimate answer and tells us where to dig on the call rather than corrupting the score with a guess.

Time needed 20–30 minutes
Who should fill this in Whoever has the clearest end-to-end view of your build and release pipeline — often a platform, DevOps or application security lead. If parts of it belong to someone else, you can answer what you know and hand the rest over: use Download / hand off at the end to pass a file to a colleague.
Nothing is submitted automatically Your answers stay in this browser tab until you choose to copy or download them at the end. The downloaded file is also how you save — nothing is stored on this page once it's closed.
